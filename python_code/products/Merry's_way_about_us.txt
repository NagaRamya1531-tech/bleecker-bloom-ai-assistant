Bleecker & Bloom is a neighborhood coffee shop in Greenwich Village, New York City.

The counter menu includes espresso drinks, coffee, drinking chocolate, pastries, and flavor additions. Ask the barista about ingredients or help choosing something from the menu.

Delivery area
Delivery is available in Greenwich Village, SoHo, West Village, and Lower Manhattan.

Saturday: 8 AM - 8 PM
