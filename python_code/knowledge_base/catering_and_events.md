# Catering, Events, and Sustainability

## Office catering
We cater breakfast meetings and events of 10 to 100 people in our delivery area. Our Coffee Traveler (96 oz, about 12 cups) of Old Time Diner Blend or Organic Decaf is $28 and comes with cups, milk, and sugar. Our Pastry Box (a dozen assorted croissants and scones) is $36. Please order at least 24 hours in advance by emailing catering@bleeckerandbloom.example or calling the shop.

## Private events
The shop can be booked for private events of up to 40 people on Sunday evenings after 6 PM. Packages start at $400 and include an espresso bar with a barista and a pastry spread.

## Latte art and coffee classes
We hold a two-hour Latte Art Basics class on the first Saturday of every month at 6 PM, and a Home Brewing class on the third Saturday at 6 PM. Classes cost $45, include all coffee and a bag of beans to take home, and are limited to 8 people. Sign up in store or by email.

## Community events
Every Thursday from 6 to 8 PM is Open Mic Night. Local artists' work hangs on our walls and changes every two months.

## Sustainability
Our cups and lids are compostable, and we compost all coffee grounds; free bags of used grounds for your garden are by the door. We buy beans from importers who pay farmers above Fair Trade minimums. Unsold pastries are donated every evening to a local food rescue program. Bring your own cup and get $0.25 off.
