# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you have Wi-Fi?
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available to all customers. The network name and password are printed on your receipt and posted by the register.

## Can I work on my laptop?
Yes. We have 32 seats, and power outlets along the window bar and the back wall. On weekends from 10 AM to 2 PM we ask laptop users to limit their stay to 90 minutes so everyone can find a seat.

## Is there seating?
We have 32 seats indoors and 8 seats on the sidewalk patio, which is open from April to October, weather permitting.

## Are dogs allowed?
Dogs are welcome on the outdoor patio, and we keep water bowls and free dog treats by the door. Only service animals are allowed indoors.

## Is the shop wheelchair accessible?
Yes. We have a step-free entrance with an automatic door, an accessible restroom, and lower counter space at the pickup area.

## Do you have restrooms?
Yes, one accessible, all-gender restroom for customers.

## Is there parking?
We do not have a parking lot. Metered street parking is available on Bleecker Street and nearby blocks, and there is a public garage on Leroy Street. We are a short walk from the Christopher St–Sheridan Sq subway station.

## Do you sell gift cards?
Yes. Digital and physical gift cards are available from $10 to $200 and never expire.

## Can I bring my own cup?
Yes! Bring a clean reusable cup and get $0.25 off any drink.

## Are you hiring?
We are often looking for baristas and bakers. Email your resume to jobs@bleeckerandbloom.example or ask for the manager in store. Barista experience is a plus but not required; we train everyone.

## Do you have a kids' menu?
Kids love a kids' size (8 oz, served warm) of our Dark Hot Chocolate or Vanilla Steamer, and our Chocolate Croissant or Blueberry Muffin.

## Do you serve tea?
Yes. We brew eight teas to order from $2.50: Earl Grey, English Breakfast, Serenity Green Tea, Peppermint, Lemon Grass, and three chais. Any chai can be made into a chai latte with steamed milk.

## Do you serve cold brew or iced drinks?
Yes. Our Cold Brew is steeped for 18 hours and made fresh every day ($4.25), and we also make a Vanilla Sweet Cream Cold Brew, Iced Latte, Iced Americano, and Iced Mocha. Any espresso drink, brewed coffee, or tea can be served iced at no extra charge.

## Do you serve smoothies or frozen drinks?
No. We do not make smoothies, juices, or blended frozen drinks.

## Do you serve food?
Yes. Besides our bakery, we serve breakfast sandwiches (Bacon, Egg & Cheese and Egg & Cheese), Everything Bagels with cream cheese, Avocado Toast, Greek Yogurt Parfaits, and Steel-Cut Oatmeal all day, and a Turkey & Cheddar Sandwich from 11 AM.
