# Brewing Guides

Tips from our baristas for brewing our beans and teas at home. A good starting ratio for most coffee methods is 1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water.

## Pour-over (V60 or Chemex)
Use 22 g of medium-fine ground coffee and 350 g of water at 200°F (93°C). Pour 50 g of water to "bloom" the grounds for 30 seconds, then pour slowly in circles until you reach 350 g. Total brew time should be 3 to 3.5 minutes. Recommended beans: Ethiopia, Columbian Medium Roast, Primo Espresso Roast.

## French press
Use 30 g of coarse ground coffee and 500 g of water just off the boil. Stir, put the lid on, and steep for 4 minutes. Press slowly and pour immediately so it does not over-extract. Recommended beans: Our Old Time Diner Blend, Brazilian - Organic, Civet Cat.

## Drip coffee maker
Use 2 tablespoons (about 10 g) of medium ground coffee per 6 oz of water. Recommended beans: Our Old Time Diner Blend, Jamacian Coffee River.

## Espresso at home
Use 18 g of finely ground coffee for a double shot, aiming for about 36 g of espresso in 25 to 30 seconds. If it runs too fast, grind finer; too slow, grind coarser. Recommended beans: Espresso Roast, Primo Espresso Roast.

## Cold brew at home
Combine 100 g of coarse ground coffee with 1 liter of cold water. Steep in the fridge for 16 to 18 hours, then strain. Dilute 1:1 with water or milk. Recommended beans: Brazilian - Organic.

## Steeping tea
Use 1 teaspoon of loose tea per 8 oz cup.
Black tea (English Breakfast, Earl Grey): water at 212°F (boiling), steep 3 to 5 minutes.
Green tea (Serenity Green Tea): water at 175°F (80°C), steep 2 to 3 minutes. Hotter water makes it bitter.
Herbal tea (Lemon Grass, Peppermint): water at 212°F, steep 5 to 7 minutes.
Chai: simmer 1 tablespoon in equal parts water and milk for 5 minutes, then sweeten to taste.

## Storing coffee beans
Keep beans in an airtight, opaque container at room temperature. Do not store them in the fridge. Coffee tastes best within 4 weeks of the roast date printed on the bag. Grind just before brewing for the freshest flavor.
