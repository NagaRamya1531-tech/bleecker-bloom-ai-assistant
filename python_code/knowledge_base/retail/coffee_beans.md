# Whole Bean Coffee

Our retail shelf carries whole bean coffee in 12 oz bags. Retail items are sold in store only and are not available through app ordering or delivery. We can grind any bag for you for free; just tell the barista your brew method.

## Espresso Roast
Price: $14.75 per 12 oz bag. Type: Espresso beans.
Our house blend for a good espresso shot, and the blend we use for every espresso drink behind the counter. A medium-dark roast of Brazilian and Colombian beans with notes of dark chocolate, toasted hazelnut, and brown sugar.
Best for: Espresso machines, moka pots.

## Primo Espresso Roast
Price: $20.45 per 12 oz bag. Type: Espresso beans.
Our premium single-origin espresso, hand roasted in small batches. A bright, fruity medium roast with notes of cherry, cacao nib, and orange peel.
Best for: Espresso, or as a bold pour-over.

## Our Old Time Diner Blend
Price: $18.00 per 12 oz bag. Type: House blend.
A full-bodied, no-fuss blend reminiscent of the cup of coffee you used to get at a classic New York diner. Medium roast with notes of toast, caramel, and walnut.
Best for: Drip coffee makers, French press. It's our house drip coffee at the counter.

## Brazilian - Organic
Price: $18.00 per 12 oz bag. Type: Organic beans. Origin: Minas Gerais, Brazil.
It's like Carnival in a cup. Clean and smooth, with low acidity and notes of milk chocolate, almond, and sweet citrus. USDA certified organic.
Best for: Drip, French press, cold brew at home. It's the bean we use for our Cold Brew.

## Columbian Medium Roast
Price: $15.00 per 12 oz bag. Type: Gourmet beans. Origin: Huila, Colombia.
A smooth, balanced cup of coffee any time of day. Notes of red apple, caramel, and cocoa.
Best for: Pour-over, drip.

## Ethiopia
Price: $21.00 per 12 oz bag. Type: Gourmet beans. Origin: Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia.
From the home of coffee. A light roast, washed-process coffee with floral jasmine aroma and notes of lemon, bergamot, and blueberry.
Best for: Pour-over (V60 or Chemex). Try it first at the counter as our Ethiopia Pour-Over.

## Jamacian Coffee River
Price: $19.75 per 12 oz bag. Type: Premium beans. Origin: Blue Mountains, Jamaica.
It will start your day off right. Mild, sweet, and remarkably smooth, with almost no bitterness. Notes of honey, milk chocolate, and herbs.
Best for: Drip, pour-over.

## Civet Cat
Price: $45.00 per 12 oz bag. Type: Premium beans. Origin: Indonesia.
A rare novelty coffee processed through the digestive system of the Asian palm civet, famous as one of the most expensive coffees in the world. Our supplier sources only from certified wild-collected, cage-free producers. Earthy and syrupy with very low acidity.
Best for: Pour-over or French press, as a special-occasion coffee.

## Organic Decaf Blend
Price: $22.50 per 12 oz bag. Type: Organic beans.
Our blend of hand-picked organic beans, naturally decaffeinated using the chemical-free Swiss Water Process (99.9% caffeine free). The same decaf we use for our Decaf Drip and decaf espresso drinks in the shop. Notes of cocoa and dried cherry.
Best for: Any brew method.

## Guatemalan Sustainably Grown
Price: $10.00 per 12 oz bag. Type: Green (unroasted) beans. Origin: Huehuetenango, Guatemala.
Green coffee beans you can roast yourself at home in a popcorn popper, oven, or home roaster. Rainforest Alliance certified.
Best for: Home roasters.
