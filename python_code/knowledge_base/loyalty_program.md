# Bloom Rewards Loyalty Program

## How it works
Bloom Rewards is our free loyalty program. Sign up in the app or at the counter with your phone number. You earn 10 points for every $1 you spend on the counter menu, in store or in the app.

## Rewards
150 points: a free flavor syrup or milk alternative.
500 points: a free pastry of your choice.
800 points: a free drink of any size.
Points expire 12 months after you earn them.

## Member perks
Members get a free drink on their birthday, early access to seasonal menu items, and double points every Tuesday.

## Tiers
Members who earn 5,000 points in a calendar year reach Gold status. Gold members earn 15 points per $1, get free delivery on every order, and receive a free bag of beans each year.

## Checking your points
Your points balance is shown on the Profile screen of the app. Points from delivery orders are added once the order is delivered. Points cannot be transferred between accounts or exchanged for cash.
