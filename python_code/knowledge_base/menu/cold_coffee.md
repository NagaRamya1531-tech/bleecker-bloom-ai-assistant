# Cold Coffee

Our cold coffee is available all year. Cold brew is made in small batches every day and can sell out by late afternoon. All cold drinks come in small, medium, or large at the same price. We do not make blended frozen drinks or smoothies.

## Cold Brew
Price: $4.25.
Our Brazilian Organic beans steeped in cold water for 18 hours, for a smooth, chocolatey, low-acid coffee served over ice. Stronger than iced drip coffee.
Ingredients: Cold Brew Coffee, Water, Ice.
Allergens: None.
Caffeine: about 200 mg (medium).
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, contains-caffeine

## Vanilla Sweet Cream Cold Brew
Price: $5.25.
Our cold brew topped with a float of house-made vanilla sweet cream that slowly cascades into the coffee.
Ingredients: Cold Brew Coffee, Cream, Milk, Vanilla Syrup, Ice.
Allergens: Dairy (milk, cream).
Caffeine: about 185 mg (medium). Calories: about 110 (medium).
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free, contains-dairy, contains-caffeine

## Iced Latte
Price: $4.75.
A double shot of espresso with cold milk over ice. Add any syrup to make it a vanilla, caramel, or hazelnut iced latte.
Ingredients: Espresso, Cold Milk, Ice.
Allergens: Dairy (milk). Can be made dairy-free with oat, almond, or soy milk.
Caffeine: about 150 mg. Calories: about 130 (medium, whole milk).
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, contains-dairy, contains-caffeine

## Iced Americano
Price: $3.75.
A double shot of espresso with cold water over ice. Crisp, refreshing, and almost calorie-free.
Ingredients: Espresso, Cold Water, Ice.
Allergens: None.
Caffeine: about 150 mg. Calories: about 10.
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, contains-caffeine

## Iced Mocha
Price: $5.50.
Espresso, our house chocolate sauce, and cold milk over ice. Whipped cream on request at no charge.
Ingredients: Espresso, Chocolate, Cold Milk, Ice.
Allergens: Dairy (milk). Our chocolate sauce is made in a facility that also handles tree nuts.
Caffeine: about 165 mg. Calories: about 260 (medium, whole milk).
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, contains-dairy, contains-caffeine
