# Tea & Tea Lattes

Our teas are steeped to order from the same loose leaf tins we sell on our retail shelf. Teas are served plain; milk, honey, and lemon are free at the condiment bar. Any tea can be served iced. Tea lattes are made with whole milk by default; oat, almond, or soy milk is $0.75 extra.
