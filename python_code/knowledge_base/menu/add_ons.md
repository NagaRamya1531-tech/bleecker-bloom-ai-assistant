# Add-ons and Drink Customization

## Flavor syrups
Each syrup is $1.50 and adds two pumps to a medium drink (one pump for small, three for large). Syrups can be added to any coffee, tea latte, hot chocolate, or steamer.

## Caramel Syrup
Price: $1.50. Rating: 4.9 out of 5.
Sweet, buttery caramel syrup. Our caramel is made with real cream and butter.
Ingredients: Sugar, Water, Cream, Butter, Vanilla Extract.
Allergens: Dairy.
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free, contains-dairy, caffeine-free

## Chocolate Syrup
Price: $1.50. Rating: 4.8 out of 5.
Rich, velvety chocolate syrup. Turns a latte into a mocha-style drink.
Ingredients: Sugar, Cocoa Powder, Water, Vanilla Extract.
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, caffeine-free

## Hazelnut Syrup
Price: $1.50. Rating: 4.7 out of 5.
Smooth, nutty sweetness. Our most popular latte add-on.
Ingredients: Sugar, Water, Hazelnut Extract, Vanilla Extract.
Allergens: Made with natural hazelnut extract; avoid if you have a tree nut allergy.
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, contains-nuts, caffeine-free

## Sugar-Free Vanilla Syrup
Price: $1.50. Rating: 4.4 out of 5.
Classic vanilla flavor with zero sugar, sweetened with sucralose. A good choice for people watching their sugar intake.
Ingredients: Water, Natural Flavors, Sucralose.
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, sugar-free, caffeine-free

## Milk alternatives
We offer oat milk, almond milk, and soy milk for $0.75 extra, as well as skim milk and half-and-half at no extra charge. Oat milk steams best and is our barista recommendation for lattes and flat whites.

## Oat Milk
Price: $0.75.
Creamy barista-edition oat milk that steams beautifully. Our barista pick for lattes, flat whites, and matcha. Not certified gluten-free.
Ingredients: Oat Milk.
Allergens: None of the major allergens.
Tags: vegan, dairy-free, nut-free, caffeine-free

## Almond Milk
Price: $0.75.
Light, unsweetened almond milk with a subtle nutty flavor.
Ingredients: Almond Milk.
Allergens: Tree nuts (almonds).
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, contains-nuts, caffeine-free

## Soy Milk
Price: $0.75.
Lightly sweetened soy milk with the most protein of our milk alternatives.
Ingredients: Soy Milk.
Allergens: Soy.
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, caffeine-free

## Extra Espresso Shot
Price: $1.00.
Add an extra shot of house espresso to any drink. Ask for "ristretto" for a sweeter, more concentrated shot, or "long" for a lighter one.
Ingredients: Espresso.
Allergens: None.
Caffeine: about 75 mg.
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, contains-caffeine

## Sweetness and temperature
We offer raw cane sugar, honey, and stevia at the condiment bar for free. Drinks can be made extra hot, warm (kid temperature), or iced at no charge.

## Sizes
Drinks come in small (8 oz), medium (12 oz), and large (16 oz). Our menu prices are the same for every size; we do not charge more for a larger cup.
