# Bakery

Everything in our bakery case is baked in house every morning, starting at 4 AM. Popular items like the Almond Croissant and Chocolate Croissant often sell out by early afternoon. Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs, and tree nuts (almonds and hazelnuts), so we cannot guarantee any pastry is free from cross-contact. Our Banana Bread is vegan. We do not currently offer gluten-free pastries; for a gluten-free breakfast, try the Steel-Cut Oatmeal or Greek Yogurt Parfait.
