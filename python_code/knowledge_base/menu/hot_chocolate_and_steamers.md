# Hot Chocolate & Steamers

Low- and no-caffeine drinks for kids, evenings, and anyone skipping coffee. Whipped cream on request at no charge.

## Dark Hot Chocolate
Price: $5.00. Rating: 4.7 out of 5.
Our signature hot chocolate, made by melting 70% dark cocoa into steamed milk. Rich, thick, and less sweet than a typical hot chocolate.
Ingredients: Cocoa Powder, Sugar, Milk.
Allergens: Dairy (milk). Can be made with oat milk for a dairy-free version.
Caffeine: about 15 mg (from cocoa), so it is a good choice for kids and evenings.
Calories: about 320 (medium, whole milk).
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free, contains-dairy, low-caffeine
Pairs well with: Chocolate Croissant, Ginger Biscotti.

## Organic Hot Chocolate
Price: $4.75.
Made with our certified organic drinking chocolate and steamed milk. Lighter and a little sweeter than our Dark Hot Chocolate.
Ingredients: Organic Cocoa, Sugar, Milk.
Allergens: Dairy (milk). Can be made with oat milk for a dairy-free version.
Caffeine: about 10 mg (from cocoa).
Calories: about 290 (medium, whole milk).
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free, contains-dairy, low-caffeine

## Vanilla Steamer
Price: $3.75.
Steamed milk with vanilla syrup. Warm, sweet, completely caffeine-free, and a kids' favorite. Swap the vanilla for any syrup at no charge.
Ingredients: Steamed Milk, Vanilla Syrup.
Allergens: Dairy (milk). Can be made dairy-free with oat, almond, or soy milk.
Caffeine: none.
Calories: about 220 (medium, whole milk).
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free, contains-dairy, caffeine-free

## Kids' hot chocolate
Ask for a kids' size (8 oz, served warm rather than hot) of the Dark Hot Chocolate, Organic Hot Chocolate, or Vanilla Steamer. It is charged at the same menu price.
