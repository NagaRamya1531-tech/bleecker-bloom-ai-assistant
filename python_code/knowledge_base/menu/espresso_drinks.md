# Espresso Drinks

All espresso drinks are made with our house Espresso Roast and come in small (8 oz), medium (12 oz), or large (16 oz) at the same price. Milk drinks are made with whole milk by default; oat, almond, or soy milk is $0.75 extra (see Add-ons).

## Espresso
Price: $2.00. Rating: 4.9 out of 5.
A bold shot of rich espresso pulled from our house Espresso Roast, with notes of dark chocolate and toasted hazelnut (a flavor note only). Order a single, or ask for a double (doppio) for $3.00.
Ingredients: Espresso.
Allergens: None.
Caffeine: about 75 mg per single shot. Calories: under 5.
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, contains-caffeine

## Ouro Brasileiro Espresso
Price: $3.00.
A premium single-origin Brazilian espresso shot, sweeter and smoother than our house espresso, with notes of caramel and cocoa. Can be used in place of house espresso in any drink for $1.00 extra.
Ingredients: Ouro Brasileiro Espresso.
Allergens: None.
Caffeine: about 75 mg per shot. Calories: under 5.
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, contains-caffeine

## Americano
Price: $3.75.
A double shot of espresso topped with hot water for a smooth, full-bodied coffee with no milk.
Ingredients: Espresso, Hot Water.
Allergens: None.
Caffeine: about 150 mg. Calories: about 10.
Tags: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, contains-caffeine

## Macchiato
Price: $3.50.
A double shot of espresso "marked" with a dollop of milk foam. Strong, with just a touch of creaminess. This is the traditional Italian macchiato, not a large layered latte.
Ingredients: Espresso, Milk Foam.
Allergens: Dairy (milk).
Caffeine: about 150 mg. Calories: about 20.
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, contains-dairy, contains-caffeine

## Cortado
Price: $4.00.
Equal parts espresso and steamed milk, served in a 4.5 oz glass. Smooth, balanced, and stronger than a latte.
Ingredients: Espresso, Steamed Milk.
Allergens: Dairy (milk). Can be made dairy-free with oat, almond, or soy milk.
Caffeine: about 150 mg. Calories: about 60.
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, contains-dairy, contains-caffeine

## Cappuccino
Price: $4.50. Rating: 4.7 out of 5.
A rich and creamy cappuccino made with a double shot of espresso, steamed milk, and a thick, frothy milk cap.
Ingredients: Espresso, Steamed Milk, Milk Foam.
Allergens: Dairy (milk). Can be made dairy-free with oat, almond, or soy milk.
Caffeine: about 150 mg. Calories: about 130 (medium, whole milk).
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, contains-dairy, contains-caffeine

## Flat White
Price: $5.00.
Espresso with steamed milk and a fine layer of microfoam for a velvety, balanced cup. A flat white has less milk than a latte, so the coffee flavor is stronger. We use a ristretto double shot for extra sweetness.
Ingredients: Espresso, Steamed Milk, Milk Foam.
Allergens: Dairy (milk). Can be made dairy-free with oat, almond, or soy milk.
Caffeine: about 130 mg. Calories: about 110 (medium, whole milk).
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, contains-dairy, contains-caffeine

## Latte
Price: $4.75. Rating: 4.8 out of 5.
Smooth and creamy: a double shot of espresso with plenty of velvety steamed milk and a thin layer of foam. Our most popular espresso drink, and a great base for flavor syrups.
Ingredients: Espresso, Steamed Milk, Milk Foam.
Allergens: Dairy (milk). Can be made dairy-free with oat, almond, or soy milk.
Caffeine: about 150 mg. Calories: about 190 (medium, whole milk).
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, contains-dairy, contains-caffeine

## Mocha
Price: $5.50.
Espresso and our house chocolate sauce blended with steamed milk for a rich, chocolatey coffee. Topped with cocoa powder. Whipped cream on request at no charge.
Ingredients: Espresso, Chocolate, Steamed Milk.
Allergens: Dairy (milk). Our chocolate sauce is made in a facility that also handles tree nuts.
Caffeine: about 165 mg (espresso plus chocolate). Calories: about 290 (medium, whole milk).
Tags: vegetarian, gluten-free, contains-dairy, contains-caffeine

## Honey Oat Latte
Price: $5.75.
Our signature latte: a double shot of espresso with steamed oat milk, local New York honey, and a dusting of cinnamon. Naturally dairy-free.
Ingredients: Espresso, Oat Milk, Honey, Cinnamon.
Allergens: None of the major allergens. Contains honey, so it is not vegan. Our oat milk is not certified gluten-free.
Caffeine: about 150 mg. Calories: about 210 (medium).
Tags: vegetarian, dairy-free, nut-free, contains-caffeine

## Decaf and iced drinks
Any espresso drink can be made decaf at no extra charge using our Organic Decaf Blend (Swiss Water Process). Any espresso drink can also be served iced at no extra charge; see the Cold Coffee menu for cold brew and our iced favorites.
