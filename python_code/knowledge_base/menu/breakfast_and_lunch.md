# Breakfast & Lunch

Breakfast sandwiches and bagels are served all day. Sandwiches and toast are made to order, so allow about 5 to 8 minutes. Our bagels and rolls come fresh every morning from a local New York bakery. Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs, soy, sesame, and tree nuts.

## Bacon, Egg & Cheese
Price: $6.50.
The New York classic: two fried eggs, crispy bacon, and American cheese on a toasted, buttered kaiser roll. Ask for it on an everything bagel for no extra charge.
Ingredients: Kaiser Roll, Eggs, Bacon, American Cheese, Butter.
Allergens: Wheat (gluten), Eggs, Dairy.
Calories: about 520.
