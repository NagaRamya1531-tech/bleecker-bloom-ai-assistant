# Delivery and Pickup

## Delivery area
Delivery is available in Greenwich Village, SoHo, West Village, and Lower Manhattan. We do not deliver outside these neighborhoods, including to Brooklyn or Midtown.

## Delivery fees and minimum
Delivery costs $2.99 per order. Orders over $25 get free delivery. The minimum order for delivery is $10.

## Delivery times
Most deliveries arrive in 20 to 35 minutes. During the morning rush (7:30 to 9:30 AM on weekdays) and in bad weather, deliveries can take up to 45 minutes. The last delivery order is accepted 30 minutes before closing.

## Pickup
Choose pickup in the app to skip the line. Pickup orders are usually ready in 5 to 10 minutes and wait for you on the shelf by the door, labeled with your name. Pickup orders are held for 30 minutes.

## What can be delivered
All counter menu items (espresso drinks, brewed and cold coffee, tea, hot chocolate, pastries, breakfast and lunch, and add-ons) can be delivered. Pour-overs and made-to-order food add about 5 minutes. Retail shelf items such as whole bean coffee, loose leaf tea, and merchandise are sold in store only.

## Keeping drinks hot
Hot drinks are delivered in insulated sleeves with spill-proof lids. Iced drinks are packed with ice on the side for orders with a delivery time over 30 minutes.
