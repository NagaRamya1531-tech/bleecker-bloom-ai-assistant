# Allergen and Dietary Information

## Allergen policy
Our kitchen and bar handle milk, wheat, eggs, soy, sesame, mustard, and tree nuts (almonds and hazelnuts). We take care to prevent cross-contact, but we cannot guarantee that any item is completely free of allergens. If you have a severe allergy, please tell your barista before ordering. Each menu item lists its allergens in the menu guides.

## Nut allergies
Items that contain tree nuts: Almond Croissant, Chocolate Chip Biscotti (almonds), Hazelnut Biscotti, Hazelnut Syrup, and Almond Milk. Our nut-free-recipe pastries and food (Croissant, Chocolate Croissant, all scones, Ginger Biscotti, Blueberry Muffin, Banana Bread, Cinnamon Roll, and all breakfast and lunch items) are made in the same kitchen as nut items. Our granola is nut-free. We do not use peanuts in any product.

## Dairy-free and vegan options
Dairy-free drinks: Espresso, Americano, Ouro Brasileiro Espresso, Honey Oat Latte, Cold Brew, Iced Americano, all brewed coffees and plain teas, and any milk drink made with oat, almond, or soy milk. Vegan syrups: Chocolate, Hazelnut, and Sugar-Free Vanilla (the Caramel Syrup contains dairy). Vegan food: Banana Bread, Avocado Toast, and Steel-Cut Oatmeal. Dairy-free pastries: Banana Bread, Hazelnut Biscotti, and Ginger Biscotti (the biscotti contain eggs).

## Gluten-free options
All of our coffee drinks, brewed coffees, teas, hot chocolates, and syrups are gluten-free (our oat milk is not certified gluten-free). All of our pastries, bagels, sandwiches, and toast contain wheat; we do not currently offer gluten-free baked goods. Gluten-free food: Steel-Cut Oatmeal and Greek Yogurt Parfait (made with certified gluten-free oats).

## Low sugar and low caffeine
Ask for the Sugar-Free Vanilla Syrup for sweetness without sugar. For no caffeine, choose Peppermint Tea, Lemon Grass Tea, or a Vanilla Steamer. For low caffeine, choose the Decaf Drip or any drink made decaf (about 5 mg), the Dark Hot Chocolate or Organic Hot Chocolate (10 to 15 mg), or Serenity Green Tea (about 30 mg). Our Cold Brew is our strongest drink (about 200 mg).

## Pregnancy and caffeine
A medium latte or cappuccino has about 150 mg of caffeine. Decaf espresso drinks have about 5 mg. We recommend checking with your doctor about daily caffeine limits.
