# Orders, Payments, and Refunds

## Payment methods
In store we accept credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash. App orders are paid by card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay. We do not accept checks or cryptocurrency.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an app order for a full refund within 2 minutes of placing it, before the barista starts making it. After that, please call the shop at (212) 555-0187 and we will do our best to help.

## Refunds and remakes
If your drink is not right, tell us and we will remake it for free; no receipt needed in store. For delivery orders that arrive wrong, missing items, or spilled, contact us within 24 hours through the app or by phone and we will refund the affected items to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

## Retail returns
Unopened retail items (beans, tea, chocolate, merchandise) can be returned within 14 days with a receipt. Opened food items cannot be returned unless they are defective.

## Tipping
Tipping is optional. All tips go directly to our baristas and delivery couriers.

## Gift cards
Digital and physical gift cards are available in any amount from $10 to $200. Gift cards never expire and can be used in store and in the app.
