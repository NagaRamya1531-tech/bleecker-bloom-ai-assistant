# About Bleecker & Bloom

## Our story
Bleecker & Bloom is a neighborhood coffee shop on Bleecker Street in Greenwich Village, New York City. We opened in 2019 with one espresso machine, a counter of morning pastries, and a simple idea: great coffee should feel personal. Every pastry is baked fresh each morning, and every espresso drink is pulled to order by our baristas.

## What we serve
Our counter menu has eight sections: espresso drinks, brewed coffee and pour-overs, cold coffee (including cold brew), tea and tea lattes, hot chocolate and steamers, freshly baked pastries, breakfast and lunch (breakfast sandwiches, bagels, avocado toast, oatmeal, and a sandwich), and add-ons like syrups and milk alternatives. Our retail shelf sells whole bean coffee, loose leaf tea, packaged drinking chocolate, and Bleecker & Bloom branded merchandise.

## Team
Our head barista, Priya, leads a team of eight baristas. Our pastries are baked every morning from 4 AM by our head baker, Marco. Ask any barista for a recommendation; they love talking about coffee.
